# ISA-101 screens

ISA-101 (high-performance HMI) exists because colourful, busy screens hide the one abnormal thing an operator must see. The theme in `hmi.css` follows it; keep screens consistent with it.

## Display levels

| Level | Shows | Typical content | Build with |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 Overview | The whole area at a glance, for the shift | Key values against their normal range, unit status, alarm counts per unit | Tiles of values with range bars; `AlarmBanner`; no P&ID |
| L2 Unit | One process unit, for running it | Equipment in process order, main flows, key values, trends | `UnitScreen` (equipment) or `PidUnit` (process view, `scada-pid`) |
| L3 Detail | One piece of equipment, for diagnosing it | Every measurement and status, interlocks, permissives | A faceplate grown into a page; more `Trend` pens |
| L4 Diagnostic | Instrument and loop internals | Tuning, raw I/O, source health | Tables; MACHHUB's data source diagnostics |

Navigation goes down by selecting (L1 tile → L2 screen → object → L3) and back up with one action. Keep the alarm banner on every level, and let each L1 tile show the worst alarm in its unit.

## Colour

- The screen is grey; equipment is drawn in greys. Running is a darker fill, stopped a lighter one: shade and shape, not red and green.
- Colour means **abnormal**. Alarm colours (critical red, high orange, medium yellow, low cyan) appear only on alarms, never on normal states, buttons or decoration.
- Every colour has a second cue for colour-blind operators: alarm priority also has a shape and a number (1 diamond, 2 triangle, 3 square, 4 circle); bad data is dashed and struck through.
- Liquid in a vessel is the one muted hue on a normal screen.
- The classic theme (`data-hmi-theme="classic"` on `<html>`) exists for plants that insist on red and green; switch the whole app, never one screen.

## Text and values

- Numbers in tabular figures with fixed decimals per tag; units beside the value in a quieter tone.
- Tag names as the plant writes them (P-101, XV-101), short and upper case.
- A value outside its normal range is shown against the range (a bar or the limits), not only as a number.
- Screen text is labels, values and actions; explanations belong in docs, not on the screen.

## Layout

- Process flows left to right, top to bottom.
- Design for the smallest panel the site uses, and test at that size.
- Leave space: an L2 screen with more than about 40 objects is two screens.
